# Module 03 — Turn rows into business metrics

## Mission

The store manager needs customer activity, revenue, and category metrics.

## Exercise 1 — Rental count per customer

Count rentals for every customer and sort from the most active customer to the least active customer.

## Exercise 2 — Deterministic sorting

Return all rentals ordered by `return_date` ascending and, for equal return dates, by `rental_date` descending. Put `NULL` return dates last.

## Exercise 3 — Payment activity

Return customers who made at least 30 payments and paid more than 200 total.

## Exercise 4 — Average film length by category

Calculate the average film length by category. Use the real many-to-many relationship through `film_category`; `film.category_id` does not exist.

## Exercise 5 — Actors with large filmographies

Return actors who appear in at least 35 films.
